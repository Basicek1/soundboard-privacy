## In-App Purchases
- **Monthly subscription**: Full access to all features, billed monthly. Automatically renews unless
cancelled before the end of the current period.
- **Yearly subscription**: Full access to all features, billed annually. Automatically renews unless
cancelled before the end of the current period.
- **Lifetime access**: One-time purchase for permanent access to all features.

Payments are processed by the store you purchased from (Apple App Store or Google Play) and charged to
that account.

### Managing and cancelling subscriptions
- **iPhone / iPad**: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → SoundBoard Pro Studio. Cancel at least 24
hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.
- **Android**: Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → SoundBoard
Pro Studio → Cancel subscription.

After cancelling, you keep full access until the end of the period you have already paid for.
Subscriptions purchased on one platform cannot be managed or cancelled from the other.
